# Galil Home Insurance — Customer Handbook (fictional sample)

## Water damage
Sudden and accidental water damage is covered on every plan: a burst pipe, a failed water heater, a washing machine or dishwasher hose that comes off. The policy pays for repairing the damage the water caused, including floors, walls and ceilings, and for locating the leak. Damage that develops slowly, such as seepage, rising damp or a leak that was left unrepaired for more than 30 days, is not covered.

## Theft and burglary
Theft is covered when there are signs of forced entry, and a police report must be filed within 48 hours. Cash is covered up to 2,000 shekels. Jewellery and watches are covered up to 10,000 shekels in total unless the customer holds a jewellery rider. Theft by a person who was let into the home, such as a contractor, is covered only on the Premium plan.

## Fire and smoke
Fire, smoke and explosion damage is covered on every plan, including the cost of cleaning soot and of temporary accommodation for up to 60 days while the home cannot be lived in. Damage from a fire started deliberately by the policyholder is excluded.

## Storm and wind
Storm damage to the roof, windows and exterior walls is covered on every plan. Garden structures such as pergolas, sheds and fences are covered only on the Plus and Premium plans, up to 15,000 shekels per event.

## Earthquake
Earthquake cover is an optional extension. It is included in the Premium plan and can be added to the Plus plan for an extra monthly fee. The earthquake deductible is 10 percent of the insured value of the building.

## Deductibles
The deductible is the part of each claim the customer pays. It is 1,500 shekels on the Basic plan, 900 shekels on the Plus plan and 400 shekels on the Premium plan. For water damage the deductible is doubled when the customer chooses a plumber who is not on the company's list.

## Plans and coverage limits
The Basic plan covers contents up to 150,000 shekels. The Plus plan covers contents up to 400,000 shekels and adds garden structures and third-party liability. The Premium plan covers contents up to 1,000,000 shekels and adds earthquake cover, theft by people let into the home, and home-office equipment up to 60,000 shekels.

## How to file a claim
A claim must be reported within 30 days of the event. The customer gives a short description, photographs of the damage and an estimate of the amount. Claims under 5,000 shekels are decided without a visit. For larger claims an appraiser visits within five working days.

## Payment times
An approved claim is paid within 14 days of approval, by bank transfer. If the company needs more documents, the 14 days start when the last document arrives. Emergency repairs approved by phone are paid directly to the service provider.

## Damage to neighbours
Third-party liability covers damage the customer's home causes to a neighbour, for example water leaking into the apartment below. It is included in the Plus and Premium plans, up to 500,000 shekels per event. On the Basic plan it is not included.

## Cancellation and changes
The customer can cancel at any time with 30 days' notice and is refunded for the unused part of the month. A change of plan takes effect on the first day of the following month. Claims for events before the change are handled under the plan that was in force on the day of the event.

## Emergency service
A 24-hour emergency line sends a plumber, electrician or locksmith. The first visit of each year is free on the Plus and Premium plans; on the Basic plan it costs 250 shekels per visit.
